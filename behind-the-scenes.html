Behind the Scenes

Making bad breath easier to understand.

The question before the image

Our current bad-breath project begins with everyday questions: Where can the smell come from? What belongs in an oral-care routine? When can an examination help?

Models instead of a wall of terminology

Tooth models, bathroom objects and concrete comparisons help us make explanations visible. The images support the idea rather than simply decorating it.
