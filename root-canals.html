Root canals

What is root canal treatment trying to achieve?

Quick explanationExplore the videosCommon questions

It treats the affected tissue inside the tooth with the aim of keeping the tooth. Cleaning the canals and sealing the tooth are parts of the same treatment.

01 · Understand

Understand the aim

Root canal treatment treats infection inside a tooth. The canals are cleaned, shaped and filled, and the tooth is sealed again.

02 · Look closer

The tooth afterwards

The final restoration is part of the plan. Depending on the condition of the tooth, a crown may be needed.

03 · Ask

For your conversation

“What will the final restoration be, and who should I contact if I have symptoms?” Contact your treating practice if symptoms persist or worsen.

A few questions you might have

What happens inside the canals?

Affected tissue is removed. The canals are cleaned, shaped and filled.

Is that the end of the restoration?

The final restoration also needs a plan. Ask whether a filling or crown is intended.
