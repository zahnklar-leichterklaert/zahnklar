Bad breath

Why does bad breath keep coming back?

Quick explanationExplore the videosCommon questions

Bad breath has several possible causes, including plaque, gum problems or a dry mouth. A fresh taste alone does not explain the cause.

01 · Understand

More than one cause

Bad breath can be related to teeth, gums, dry mouth, food or smoking, among other causes. Smell alone does not identify the cause.

02 · Look closer

What care includes

Alongside your teeth, clean between them and gently clean your tongue. If bad breath persists for weeks, have it checked by a dentist.

03 · Ask

Related viewing

The video below explains why oral care is an ongoing routine. Behind the Scenes shows how we are developing the new bad-breath film.

A few questions you might have

Which areas need cleaning?

Your teeth, the spaces between them and your tongue. Clean the tongue gently without injuring it.

When should I get it checked?

If it persists for weeks despite cleaning, or your gums are painful, swollen or bleeding.
