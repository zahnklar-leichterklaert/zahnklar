Whitening

Will whitening lighten my crowns too?

Quick explanationExplore the videosCommon questions

Whitening changes the colour of natural teeth. Existing crowns and other artificial restorations do not lighten to match. That needs to be considered before treatment.

01 · Understand

Clarify the goal

Whitening lightens natural teeth. Crowns and other artificial restorations do not lighten in the same way.

02 · Look closer

Check first

Teeth and gums should be checked before whitening. Sensitive teeth and irritated gums can occur as side effects.

03 · Ask

Put the options in perspective

The video compares toothpaste, strips and in-office whitening. Take this question with you: “What change is realistic for my teeth?”

A few questions you might have

What should be checked first?

Whether your teeth and gums are healthy and what colour change is appropriate for your situation.

What side effects are possible?

Teeth can become sensitive and gums may become irritated. Discuss troublesome symptoms with your dental practice.
