ABOUT ZAHNKLAR

Dentistry you can make sense of.

Benedict Lersch · Dentist and founder of Zahnklar

I want to show dentistry in a way that keeps you curious and makes sense. Including if the subject has never felt approachable, or you feel uneasy about your next dental visit.

Zahnklar brings together my work as a dentist and my love of explaining things. Videos, models and concrete comparisons make procedures visible and give everyday questions room to be explored.

What you’ll find here

Treatment explanations for adults, stories and activities for children, and resources for clearer patient conversations. Start with a video, read about a topic or try a free sample.

How to use the explanations

Models and visualisations help you see individual steps. They may be simplified. The information supports understanding; it cannot replace an examination or individual advice from your treating dental team.

A question about Zahnklar?

For questions about the content, resources or working together, you can reach me by email. For symptoms and personal treatment questions, contact your treating dental practice.
