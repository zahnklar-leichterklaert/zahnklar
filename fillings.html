Fillings

Does every early sign of decay need a filling?

Quick explanationExplore the videosCommon questions

Not always. One important distinction is whether a cavity has formed. Early changes can be treated differently from a tooth with a hole.

01 · Understand

When a filling may help

A cavity caused by decay may need a filling. Early decay without a cavity can sometimes be treated without drilling. An examination tells these situations apart.

02 · Look closer

Look closer in the videos

Our examples show damaged teeth and their restoration. Compare the starting point with the result — a single video case is not a treatment recommendation for your tooth.

03 · Ask

A question for your appointment

“What makes a filling suitable for my tooth, and what are the alternatives?”

A few questions you might have

Why does pain alone not decide treatment?

Early decay may cause no symptoms. An examination identifies what needs treatment.

What should I ask at my appointment?

“Can you show me where the damage is and explain why you recommend this treatment?”
